## `01 / WHO IS THIS`

I'm a biomedical engineering student interested in what happens when you put a **human being inside a machine designed to operate at extreme speed.**

My work sits somewhere between:

`BIOMECHANICS` · `THERMAL SYSTEMS` · `HUMAN FACTORS` · `MOTORSPORT` · `GLOVES N STUFF`

I like engineering problems that become significantly more interesting when a person is involved.

And yes, I will probably turn the problem into an F1 analogy.
